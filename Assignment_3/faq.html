FAQs

Everything you need to know before Saturnalia.

Who can participate in Saturnalia?

Students from colleges and universities across India can participate in Saturnalia.

When will Saturnalia take place?

Saturnalia 2026 will be held in November 2026.

Can students participate in multiple events?

Yes, students can participate in multiple events, subject to the individual event schedules.

Are team events available?

Yes. Events such as Sat Hack, Battle of Bands and group dance competitions require teams.

How do I register?

Visit the Register page, fill in your details and select the competition you wish to participate in.

Where will the events take place?

Events will be conducted at different venues across the Thapar University campus.
